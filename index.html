Goodwill Opportunity Accelerator® (OA)
Theory of Action

A Clear Path to Consistent, High-Quality Career Services in Every Community
Goodwill Industries International
Together, we've built a shared vision. Now we need to build the path — so every person who walks through a Goodwill door gets the support they need to launch a career, achieve stability and keep growing.
The Challenge

150+ organizations. No standard path.

Quality depends too much on which door someone walks through. The OA was designed to change that.
🏘️

Unequal Access

Organizations with the most resources provide the most. Communities with the greatest need often have the fewest tools. There is no standard of services across the network.

🚧

The Real Barriers

  • Staff Capacity. Staff wear too many hats. Career Navigators are pulled into data entry, outreach and training — leaving less time for the people they serve.
  • Organizational Systems. Without consistent intake processes, assessment tools and data tracking, organizations can't see what's working or where people are falling through the cracks.
  • Change Management. Moving from informal practices to a structured model takes time, clarity and support. Organizations need to know exactly what to change, in what order and who owns each part.

GII Support Model

Matched to where Goodwill organizations are.

🌱

Self-Directed

Tiers A–B

At their own pace with GII-provided tools.• OA Landing Page on Goodwill Exchange
• OA Readiness Assessment
• OA Logic Model and Fidelity Index
• Draft an OA Action Plan independently
• OA Learning Events and OA Fellows

🤝

Group Advisory

Tiers B–C

Structured guidance with built-in accountability.• 2-month advisory consultation focused on closing key gaps
• OA Action Plan with clear next steps and follow-up cadence

🎯

Individualized

Tiers C–E

Hands-on partnership tailored to their organization.• 4–6 month mission consultation for organization-specific gaps
• Fidelity Monitoring to validate Gold or Platinum certification
• OA Action Plan with clear next steps and follow-up cadence

How Organizations Connect to Support

Three steps from first contact to the right level of help.

📧

Step 1: Pre-Engagement

Email outreach with OA success stories and real data. Discovery webinars. OA Fellows invitations. CEO and mission leader engagement.

📊

Step 2: Discovery Call

Review an adoption dashboard built from survey data. Understand current programming. Identify areas meeting OA requirements and key gaps. Prioritize and create an initial action plan.

🤝

Step 3: Match to Support

Based on the discovery call, connect the organization to the right level of GII support — self-directed, group advisory or individualized consultation.
