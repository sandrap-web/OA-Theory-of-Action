Build Your Foundation
Organizational Prep · KSDAs 1 to 6
  • Staff organized by grant
  • No career navigator training or OA champion
  • Referrals and employer contact are informal
  • No consistent data
  • Leaders see the value of functional staffing, career navigator certification, and OA champion, but there is no plan yet
  • A few informal partners
  • Data is inconsistent
  • Functional roles and shared entry-point forming
  • Some career navigators and OA Fellow enrolled in training
  • Partnerships being documented, LMI use starting, CMS in place
  • Staff organized by function
  • Navigators certified, at least one OA Fellow
  • Formal referral and employer partnerships
  • Participant data tracked in a case management system, with follow-up at 30, 60, 90, and 180 days after placement
  • Full functional model
  • Ongoing staff development, two or more Fellows
  • Data-driven partnerships, and employers co-create training
  • 365-day data drives improvement
  • Monthly data reporting to GII
🔍
Meet People Where They Are
Assess & Plan · KSDAs 7 to 11
  • Passive outreach
  • No standard intake, assessment, orientation, or plan
  • Single-channel outreach
  • Intake, assessments, and orientation vary by staff
  • Plans are partial and/or written by staff
  • Recruitment strategy forming
  • Standard intake and assessment tools emerging
  • Most participants get orientation and a plan, but plans don't guide services
  • Every participant gets a consistent intake, whole-person assessment, orientation, and an ICFP built with them using SMART goals
  • Multi-channel outreach encouraged
  • Outreach through partners and referral technology
  • Ongoing re-assessment
  • Orientation improved with feedback
  • ICFP is a living plan that drives all services
📚
Deliver the Right Services
Equip & Connect · KSDAs 12 to 18
  • Barriers go unaddressed
  • No training, financial education, career exploration, or job search help
  • Informal supports and training on request only
  • No standard curriculum and no link to individual needs or employer demand
  • Defined supports and training offerings reach many participants but aren't matched to needs or employer feedback yet
  • Training being reviewed against local demand
  • Every participant can access needs-based supports, financial education, foundational and digital skills, in-demand credential training, career exploration, and job search prep
  • Proactive, whole-person support
  • Employer-shaped, leveled training leads to credentials and living-wage jobs
  • Paid work-based learning prioritized
🚀
Launch Careers, Stay Connected
Launch & Evaluate · KSDAs 19 to 21
  • No placement support or follow-up
  • Contact ends at exit
  • Job leads shared informally
  • Check-ins only when time allows
  • No alumni plan
  • Placement services becoming consistent, but wages not tracked
  • Follow-up scheduled but mostly status checks
  • Alumni list forming
  • Placement help for every participant
  • Follow-up at 30, 60, 90, and 180 days tied to goals
  • At least one alumni method beyond one year (encouraged)
  • Active job development at a living wage
  • 365-day follow-up with coaching
  • Sustained alumni network with peer mentors
💡
Important: Most organizations will be at different stages for different rows — and that's completely normal.
